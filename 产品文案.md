## One-liner

Turn a photo into a stitchable cross-stitch pattern pack: a paginated symbol chart PDF, the DMC floss list with stitch counts and skein estimates, and a CSV grid — with every stitch recounted and proven before you download.

## The problem

Photo-to-pattern sites either lock you into a subscription, cap your size, watermark the chart, or produce a confetti mess of 90 colours with no usable floss list. And when a pattern's counts are wrong, you find out three weeks in, mid-face.

## What it does

- One photo in (JPG/PNG), a complete pattern pack out — no account, no upload, runs entirely on your PC
- Built-in palette of **422 DMC colours** (RGB values from the public DMC conversion chart); every cell is matched to the nearest real DMC code — the code you can actually buy
- **Colour cap you control** (12–60): least-used colours are merged into their nearest neighbours, so kits stay sane instead of exploding
- Paginated symbol chart PDF: numbered rows/columns, bold every-10 gridlines, centre cross marked, cover page with finished sizes — built for home printing at 100%
- Legend with per-colour stitch counts and skein estimates (labelled as estimates, with the assumption printed)
- `pattern.csv` grid of DMC codes for spreadsheet or chart-software workflows
- **Verification layer:** the grid is recounted from scratch — total cells must equal width × height exactly, every legend count must match its recount, and symbols must be one-to-one. The report prints the numbers

## Measured (seeded acceptance test, 11/11 checks passed)

- A seeded 8×6 photo built from four exact palette colours produced the exact expected grid (DMC 310 / B5200 / 321 / 700), 12 stitches each, conservation 48 = 8×6 to the stitch
- Colour cap 4→2 merged correctly with totals preserved; PDF pack generated with cover + chart + legend pages

## Designed for the big piece you actually want to stitch

Free web converters are fine for a 40-stitch bookmark. StitchChart is built for the other end: large, multi-page charts with page-by-page printing, full floss accounting, and patterns you can sell — the pack includes the CSV and the counts a marketplace listing needs.

## Honest limits

- Floss RGB values are screen approximations from the public chart — always buy by DMC code, not by screen colour
- Skein estimates assume ~1,800 stitches per skein (2 strands, 14-count) and are labelled estimates
- Detail smaller than one stitch is averaged into its cell; portraits want a larger stitch width
